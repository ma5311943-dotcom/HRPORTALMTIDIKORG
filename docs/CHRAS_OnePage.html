01 / PurposeMotive

One accountable system for constituency appointments. CHRAS replaces scattered registers and manual files with a single digital record from candidate enrolment to verification, seat allocation, official appointment letter and cancellation. It prevents duplicate active appointments, enforces role-based authority, and gives every candidate a read-only portal for their own record.

03 / WorkflowComplete Flow

  1. 1

    Apply or register

    Candidate signs up online, or an officer registers them with CNIC, education, photo and password. Registering roles.

  2. 2

    Check and verify

    Officer checks identity and documents. Officer enrolment saves Verified; self-signup starts Registered. Registering roles.

  3. 3

    Allocate a seat

    Select candidate, district, constituency, role and tenure. Duplicate active seats are blocked. Admin, MNA, MPA, Chairperson, Civil Officer.

  4. 4

    Issue appointment letter

    System creates the official reference and Gazette-style letter; candidate becomes Allocated.

  5. 5

    Candidate views result

    The candidate logs in to a read-only portal and sees their own record, seat and letter. Candidate only.

  6. 6

    Cancel with reason

    Appointment becomes Revoked, candidate returns to Verified, and the seat is freed. Admin, Chairperson, Civil Officer.

  7. 7

    Report and coordinate

    Dashboards show quotas and progress; reports can be generated and staff can message each other.

Demo access

Officer username and password use the role key: admin / admin, mna / mna, chairperson / chairperson, staff / staff. Candidate login uses CNIC and the password set at enrolment, or the 13 CNIC digits for demo records.

Before production

The current build is a working demo. Real deployment needs officer SSO/authentication, server-side role checks, a production database, document storage, NADRA integration, audit logs, backups and HTTPS.
